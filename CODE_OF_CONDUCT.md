# Code of conduct

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private data are not tolerated.

Privacy is especially important in this project: never pressure someone to post
transcripts, hook payloads, account names, serial numbers, or unredacted local
paths to receive support. Maintainers may remove content or participation that
puts people or their devices at risk.

Report sensitive conduct or security concerns privately through the repository's
GitHub reporting features.
